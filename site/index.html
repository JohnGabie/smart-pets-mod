SMART PETS

A companion that thinks.

Dogs (and later cats) with judgment, personality, genetics and a bond that grows. A fan-made concept for Minecraft Java.

Honest status: this is a concept in pre-alpha. There is no playable build and nothing to download yet. Everything on this page is a sketch of the idea.

Why a smarter pet?

What tends to bug players about the vanilla wolf, and what this project wants to change.

The vanilla wolf

  • Walks into lava or off a cliff
  • Attacks almost anything, even your friends
  • Dies too easily
  • Teleports to you instead of walking to you
  • Not much to do together in singleplayer

The Smart Pets idea

  • Judgment: it backs away from lava, cliffs and creepers
  • Safe by design: it never attacks you or your allies
  • A bond that grows: do things together and it trusts you more
  • No teleporting: it follows you on its own four legs
  • A real individual: no two dogs are alike

These are design goals, not features you can play today.

What the dog is meant to do

Everything below is planned. Nothing is playable yet.

Judgment

It avoids lava, cliffs and creepers about to blow, and it never attacks you or your allies. It only goes after what you mark.

Personality and genetics

Every dog is different. Traits are inherited, litters are usually 3 to 5 puppies, and each coat is pixel art mixed from the parents.

Teach it your words

A cue can be your voice, a chat word or a key, in any language. Say its name first, then the command. If it does not understand, it tilts its head. Voice is optional and still to be proven.

Home and routine

A bed is home. Dogs settle down step by step and greet you at the door. The world only lets you sleep through the night when your pets are asleep, and you can switch that off in the config.

Life stages

Puppy, adolescent, adult, senior. Age follows the days of your world, not the real clock.

Wild friends

Win a wild dog over with patience, no fighting needed. Or find an orphan puppy that needs looking after.

Skills and mastery

Combat, Exploration and Domestic skill trees. Practice makes it better, and skills only get rusty after a very long time unused.

Safe and fair

Difficulty decides whether a dog can be lost for good: it gets knocked out first and you get a window to rescue it (Hardcore has no second chance). No chores, ever.

Cats, later

Cats are planned for V2: independent, fond of naps and warm spots, and friendly on their own terms.

Meet the life stages

Four ages, drawn as little pixel dogs. Age follows the days of your world.

  1. Puppy

    Small and curious. Tires out fast, recharges fast, and hates being alone.

  2. Adolescent

    Lanky, with a big pool of energy and the odd case of the zoomies.

  3. Adult

    Steady and capable. Old enough to have litters of its own.

  4. Senior

    A grey muzzle and a smaller energy pool. Slower, but still your dog.

Inspiration

Ideas borrowed with respect. Text only: no footage or art from these works is used here.

D-Dog (Metal Gear Solid V)

Doing things together raises the bond, and the bond unlocks what your companion will do for you.

Sam (I Am Legend)

Shared routines and quiet presence: waiting at the door, small reactions that make a home feel lived in.

Togo

A hard-to-handle puppy who turns out to be a natural leader. A strong temperament is potential, not a flaw.

Real wolf packs

A wolf pack is a family, not a ladder of alphas. Leadership grows from care and experience.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

When is it out?

There is no release date. The project is in pre-alpha and nothing is playable yet. Watch the GitHub page for news.

Which Minecraft version?

The target is Minecraft Java 26.x (Fabric). That is a plan, and it can change.

Do I need a microphone?

No. Voice is optional and only planned if it proves to work well. Chat words and keys will always work.

Singleplayer or multiplayer?

Singleplayer comes first. Multiplayer is planned for V3.

Will it be free?

That is the plan: it is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Can dogs die?

It depends on the difficulty. A dog is knocked out first and you get a rescue window whose length depends on the difficulty. Hardcore has no second chance. The exact numbers are still being tuned, and the spec has the details.

Are you affiliated with Mojang?

No. Smart Pets is an independent fan-made concept, not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft. Minecraft is their trademark.
